The Company

Our Mission

We aim to provide forensic and social justice workers with an accurate, portable, and non-invasive solution to quantitatively measure the story of a bruise, with an emphasis on overcoming racial disparities by ensuring effectiveness across all skin tones.

Building Traction with Time

Over the years, our team:
  • received IRB approval for pre-clinical imaging in collaboration with the UCI Trauma Center
  • secured $73k in pre-seed funding
  • earned top placements in university-level and international competitions, and
  • completed over 60 customer discovery interviews across law enforcement, healthcare, and abuse shelters.

Acknowledgements

Since the beginning, we have had the luxury of being supported by a wide network of students, mentors, and organizations. Although we are unable to acknowledge each and every person by name, we are eternally grateful for your shared knowledge, advice, and commitment. Thank you for being a part of our story.

We do want to highlight our clinical personnel, specifically Nicholas Truong and Glen Nie, who are clinical research associates under Dr. Nahmias at the UCI Trauma Center. These two gentlemen were with us from the start of our clinical work, and were the backbone of our data collection procedures and guidelines. We cannot thank these two enough for their time and excitement with our work, and for making our preclinical dreams a true reality.

We also want to give very warm thanks to the staff at the Beckman Laser Institute, the UCI IRB board, and the BEAMS Lab, who helped us tremendously with the paperwork and logistics behind the clinical protocols.
